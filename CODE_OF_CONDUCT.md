# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous
community. Be considerate, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and
make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing
another person's private information without permission.

Technical disagreement is expected. Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
Trading-related discussions must also respect applicable law, platform rules,
and the project's prohibited-conduct boundaries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled
as privately and consistently as practical.

For conduct involving a security vulnerability, use the private reporting path
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other conduct concerns, contact a
maintainer through the repository without posting sensitive personal details
publicly.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
